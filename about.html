About DesignDay

Built by volunteers.
Driven by community.

A curated, free monthly design meetup that ran 35 editions across Bengaluru, Mumbai, and Delhi — from October 2012 to May 2017.

What

A room full of designers, every month.

DesignDay was a curated monthly gathering for designers across disciplines. Each edition followed the same format: a design story to get inspired, a skill talk, a hands-on jam, then lunch and real conversation. Entry was ₹100 — just enough to keep it serious.

No sponsors controlling the agenda. No sales pitches. Just designers getting better together.

Why

India needed a design community that showed up.

In 2012, India's design ecosystem was fragmented. There were talented designers everywhere — but no regular space where they could come together, share work, learn from each other, and push the craft forward.

DesignDay was founded to be that space. Every third Saturday, for five years, it happened. Talks on typography, product thinking, motion, branding, furniture, illustration, UX research, and everything in between. Hands-on workshops. Honest critique. Community that stuck around long after the event ended.

"Design in India doesn't have a shortage of talent. It has a shortage of community. DesignDay was our attempt to fix that — one Saturday at a time."
— Aashish Solanki, Founder

Who

Aashish Solanki

Aashish Solanki is an award-winning product designer and design entrepreneur with over 16 years of experience working with Fortune 500 companies and startups across the world. As the Founder and Design Director of NetBramha Studios — one of India's most respected design consultancies — Aashish has helped brands like Google, Microsoft, Unilever, and Cisco rethink how they connect with over a billion users.

A relentless advocate for India's design ecosystem, he founded DesignDay.co in 2012 as a monthly gathering to inspire, upskill, and connect designers across disciplines. He teaches design thinking at IIM Bangalore, has spoken at TEDx, UXIndia, and MetaRefresh, and was awarded the Eminent Engineer Award by the Chief Minister of Karnataka.
