THE EXPERIENCE

What Your Day Looks Like

Every Design Day is thoughtfully curated to balance inspiration, practical learning, hands-on collaboration, and meaningful conversations—creating an experience that stays with you long after the event ends.

01

Design Story

Hear the stories behind real products, career journeys, lessons learned, and the decisions that shaped them.

02

Design Talk

Learn through practical tutorials, case studies, and expert sessions covering UX, UI, AI, research, product thinking, and emerging trends.

03

Design Activity

Put ideas into practice through collaborative workshops, design challenges, critiques, and interactive exercises.

04

Lunch + Networking

Connect with fellow designers, students, founders, and industry leaders over conversations that often become the most valuable part of the day.
