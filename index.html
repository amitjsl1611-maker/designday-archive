The Experience

It was never just another meetup.

Talks, workshops, experiments, debates and plenty of hands-on making. Every edition had its own way of bringing designers together.

Design Activities From rapid sketching to hands-on prototyping — every edition pushed designers to make, not just think.
Talks & Conversations Practitioners sharing real work, real failures, and honest perspectives from across the design industry.
Workshops Focused sessions where designers learned new craft — typography, research, motion, and more — led by peers.
Community A space where designers from different studios, backgrounds, and cities found their people.
